## The idea

Paper business cards haven't really changed in centuries: a name, a number, a logo, and once printed they can't be updated.

**Click Card** turns a business card into a doorway to digital content. A business uploads whatever it wants to show (text, images, videos) and gets a **unique QR code** to print on its card. Anyone who scans the code sees that content, and the owner can update it at any time without reprinting a single card.

## Features

- **Upload content**: images, videos and company details
- **Public or private cards**: public cards open for anyone; private cards are password-protected
- **Unique QR code** generated for each card
- **Scan to view**: the QR code opens the card's content
- **Editable**: change the content later, and the printed card stays the same

## Tech

- Python
- Tkinter (desktop interface)
- QR code generation
